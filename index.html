Institutional Overview

Established on 17 June 2020 under the principles of English Common Law, the Institute operates as an independent, decentralized international research body. Founded by Professor Alexander James Scott Douglas and Senate Secretary Christine Leigh Muir, the programme is dedicated to translating advanced economic, ethical, and diplomatic strategy into universally accessible frameworks.

Our research focuses on the mechanics of societal credit, wealth floors, and systemic poverty eradication, ensuring that the foundational machinery of resource allocation directly serves the democratic mandate of the public trust.
